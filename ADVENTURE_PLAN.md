#Game story idea
##The player will play as a worker of the walmart.
He can choose difference story path and have difference choices of task. 
those selections will lead to different results


# 1 Path

## Decision 1
The checkout line becomes very long.

### Choices:
- Work fast to help customers
- Call another worker for help

## Decision 2
A customer cannot find a product.

### Choices:
- Help the customer
- Tell them to ask someone else



# 2nd Path - Manager

## Decision 1
Employees are struggling on a busy day.

### Choices:
- Help employees in their tasks
- Focus on store profits

## Decision 2
Customer complains about the store

### Choices:
- Solve the issue calmly
- Ignore the complaint



# 3 Path - Stock Associate

## Decision 1 
New stock arrived at store

### Choices:
- Organize products carefully
- Complete the work quickly, ignoring efficiency

## Decision 2
A coworker asks for help moving boxes.

### Choices:
- Help the coworker
- Complete your own tasks
